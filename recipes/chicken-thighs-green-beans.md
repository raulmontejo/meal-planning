# Baked Chicken Thighs & Green Beans

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 35 min · **Type:** dinner

## Ingredients
- 1.5 lb chicken thighs, bone-in or boneless
- 2-3 cups green beans, trimmed
- 2 tbsp garlic-infused oil, divided
- Salt & pepper

## Steps
1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Season chicken thighs with salt, pepper, and half the garlic-infused oil.
2. Roast 25-30 min (bone-in) or 20-22 min (boneless) until cooked through.
3. In the last 15 min, toss green beans with remaining oil, salt, and pepper, and roast alongside on a second tray.

## Notes
Garlic-infused oil instead of cloves — swap for real minced garlic if that's not an issue for you.
