# Coconut Milk & Pumpkin Seed Bowl

**Serves:** 1 · **Time:** 5 min (once chilled) · **Type:** breakfast

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup canned coconut milk, chilled
- 2 tbsp pumpkin seeds
- 1/4 tsp cinnamon
- Optional: a few berries

## Steps
1. Chill the can of coconut milk overnight so it separates and thickens.
2. Whisk or shake the thickened portion until smooth.
3. Spoon into a bowl, top with pumpkin seeds and cinnamon.

## Notes
This is the fastest breakfast in the set — good for a morning with no prep time at all. Doesn't keep well pre-assembled (seeds go soft), so whisk and top fresh each time; the coconut milk itself keeps for days once opened.
