# Chicken & Romaine Salad

**Serves:** 1 · **Time:** 10 min (with pre-cooked chicken) · **Type:** lunch

## Ingredients
- 2 cups chopped romaine
- 6 oz cooked chicken breast, sliced
- 1/4 cup walnuts, toasted
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Salt & pepper

## Steps
1. Toast walnuts in a dry pan over medium heat, 2-3 min, watching closely.
2. Toss romaine with olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
3. Top with sliced chicken and toasted walnuts.

## Notes
Built around batch-cooked chicken — see `batch-prep-notes.md`. Without pre-cooked chicken, add ~15 min to sear a breast fresh.
