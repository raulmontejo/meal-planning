# Coconut-Milk Chia Pudding

**Serves:** 4 (batch) · **Time:** 5 min hands-on + overnight chill · **Type:** breakfast

## Ingredients
- 2 cups canned coconut milk
- 1/2 cup chia seeds
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- Optional: a few raspberries per serving

## Steps
1. Whisk coconut milk, chia seeds, and cinnamon together in a jar or container.
2. Refrigerate at least 4 hours, ideally overnight — it thickens as the chia seeds absorb liquid.
3. Stir before serving; top with raspberries if using.

## Notes
Makes a full batch in one go and keeps 4-5 days refrigerated — genuinely grab-and-go once it's made. Good candidate for the Sunday batch-prep session.
