# Zucchini Omelet with Mashed Avocado

**Serves:** 1 · **Time:** 10 min · **Type:** breakfast

## Ingredients
- 2-3 eggs
- 1/2 cup zucchini, thinly sliced or shredded
- 1/2 avocado
- Squeeze of lemon juice
- Olive oil, salt & pepper

## Steps
1. Mash avocado with lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Set aside.
2. Heat olive oil in a nonstick pan. Cook zucchini until just tender, 2-3 min.
3. Beat eggs, pour over the zucchini, and cook like a standard omelet — let it set on the bottom, fold once.
4. Top with the mashed avocado just before serving.

## Notes
The avocado plays the role cream cheese or goat cheese would — creamy and a little tangy, without the dairy.
