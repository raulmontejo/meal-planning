# Turkey & Veggie Skillet with Fried Egg

**Serves:** 1-2 · **Time:** 10 min (with pre-cooked turkey & veg) · **Type:** dinner

## Ingredients
- 6-8 oz cooked ground turkey (from batch prep)
- 1.5 cups roasted vegetables (from batch prep)
- 1-2 eggs
- 1 tbsp oil
- Salt & pepper, chili flakes optional

## Steps
1. Heat turkey and roasted vegetables together in a skillet until warmed through, 4-5 min.
2. In a separate small pan, fry the egg(s) to your liking.
3. Plate the turkey-veg mix, top with the fried egg. Season and add chili flakes if you want heat.

## Notes
This is the fastest dinner in the set when the batch components are already made — mostly a reheat-and-fry job.
