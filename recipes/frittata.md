# Bell Pepper & Spinach Frittata

**Serves:** 4 (good for make-ahead) · **Time:** 30 min · **Type:** breakfast

## Ingredients
- 8 eggs
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 2 cups spinach
- 2-3 scallions, green parts only, sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt & pepper

## Steps
1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Heat olive oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium heat.
2. Sauté bell pepper 4-5 min, add spinach and scallion greens, cook until wilted.
3. Beat eggs with salt and pepper, pour over the vegetables, stir once to distribute.
4. Cook on the stovetop 2-3 min until the edges set, then transfer to the oven for 12-15 min until fully set.
5. Slice into wedges.

## Notes
Unlike the other breakfasts, this one is worth making in the full batch and eating cold or reheated across several mornings — it's the one breakfast that's genuinely make-ahead. Keeps 4 days refrigerated.
