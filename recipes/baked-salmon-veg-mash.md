# Baked Salmon, Roasted Veg & Cauliflower Mash

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 20-25 min (with roasted veg & mash on hand) · **Type:** dinner

## Ingredients
- 2 salmon fillets
- 2 cups roasted vegetables (from batch prep — broccoli, Brussels sprouts, asparagus, zucchini)
- 1-2 cups cauliflower mash (from batch prep)
- Olive oil, salt, pepper, lemon

## Steps
1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Pat salmon dry, season with salt, pepper, and a little olive oil.
2. Bake 12-15 min until it flakes easily.
3. Reheat the roasted veg and cauliflower mash while the salmon cooks (microwave or a low oven).
4. Plate together, finish salmon with a squeeze of lemon.

## Notes
Salmon doesn't reheat well, so it's the one thing here worth cooking fresh each time — the veg and mash are the batched parts. Total active time is mostly just the salmon.
