# Shrimp & Zucchini-Noodle Scampi

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 15 min · **Type:** dinner

## Ingredients
- 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 medium zucchini, spiralized into noodles
- 2 tbsp garlic-infused oil
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1/4 tsp chili flakes
- Salt & pepper

## Steps
1. Heat garlic-infused oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add shrimp, cook 2 min per side until pink and opaque. Remove and set aside.
3. Add zucchini noodles to the same skillet, toss 2-3 min until just tender — don't overcook or they turn watery.
4. Return shrimp to the pan, add lemon juice and chili flakes, toss to combine.

## Notes
Garlic-infused oil gives the garlic flavor without whole cloves — swap in real minced garlic if that's not a trigger for you. Best cooked fresh; shrimp turns rubbery reheated.
