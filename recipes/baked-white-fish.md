# Baked White Fish & Roasted Brussels Sprouts

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 20 min · **Type:** dinner

## Ingredients
- 1 lb white fish fillets (cod or tilapia)
- 2 cups Brussels sprouts, halved
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 lemon
- Fresh herbs (parsley, dill, or whatever's on hand)
- Salt & pepper

## Steps
1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss Brussels sprouts with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast 20-25 min, tossing halfway.
2. Meanwhile, pat fish dry, season with salt and pepper, drizzle with remaining olive oil.
3. Bake fish alongside the sprouts for the last 10-12 min, until it flakes easily.
4. Finish both with a squeeze of lemon and chopped herbs.

## Notes
White fish is quick and mild — good for whoever's less into a "fishy" flavor. Cook fresh; doesn't hold up as leftovers.
