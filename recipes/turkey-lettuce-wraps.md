# Turkey & Avocado Lettuce Wraps

**Serves:** 1-2 · **Time:** 10 min (with pre-cooked turkey) · **Type:** lunch

## Ingredients
- 8-10 oz cooked ground turkey (plain, from batch prep)
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 1 tbsp mustard
- 6-8 large lettuce leaves (butter or romaine, for wrapping)

## Steps
1. Reheat the ground turkey if needed.
2. Lay out lettuce leaves, spoon turkey into each, top with avocado slices and a dab of mustard.
3. Wrap and eat.

## Notes
This is really an assembly job, not a cook — the whole point of having plain-cooked ground turkey on hand. Swap mustard for hot sauce or a squeeze of lime for variety.
