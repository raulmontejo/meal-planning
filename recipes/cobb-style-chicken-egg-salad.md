# Chopped Chicken & Egg Salad

**Serves:** 1 · **Time:** 10 min (with pre-cooked chicken & eggs) · **Type:** lunch

## Ingredients
- 6 oz cooked chicken, chopped
- 2 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
- 2 cups mixed greens or chopped romaine
- 1/4 cup walnuts
- 1 tbsp mustard
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt & pepper

## Steps
1. Whisk mustard, vinegar, olive oil, salt, and pepper into a dressing.
2. Toss greens with the dressing.
3. Top with chopped chicken, egg, and walnuts.

## Notes
A dairy-free take on a Cobb — walnuts and the mustard-vinegar dressing carry the richness blue cheese usually would.
