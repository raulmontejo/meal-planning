# Chicken & Sweet Potato Hash

**Serves:** 1-2 · **Time:** 10 min (with leftover chicken & sweet potato) · **Type:** breakfast

## Ingredients
- 1-2 cups leftover grilled chicken, diced
- 1 cup leftover cooked sweet potato, diced
- 1-2 eggs
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- A spoonful of mustard-vinegar shake (mustard + vinegar, shaken together) or a squeeze of lemon
- Salt & pepper

## Steps
1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add diced chicken and sweet potato, cook until crisped and heated through, 5-6 min, stirring occasionally.
3. Push the hash to one side of the pan, crack in the egg(s), and fry or scramble to your liking.
4. Plate together, finish with a drizzle of the mustard-vinegar shake.

## Notes
Built to use up leftovers rather than a fresh cook — good the night after grilling chicken and a starchy side. Sweet potato isn't strictly low-carb, but it's a whole food and a fine occasional swap when that's what's on hand.
