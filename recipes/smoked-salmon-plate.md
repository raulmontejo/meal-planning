# Smoked Salmon Plate

**Serves:** 1 · **Time:** 5 min · **Type:** breakfast

## Ingredients
- 3-4 oz smoked salmon
- 1/2 avocado, mashed with a squeeze of lemon
- 1/2 cucumber, sliced into rounds
- Salt & pepper, capers if on hand

## Steps
1. Mash avocado with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
2. Arrange smoked salmon, cucumber rounds, and mashed avocado together — spread avocado over cucumber slices, top with salmon.

## Notes
No-cook. Mashed avocado stands in for the cream cheese this would normally use.
