# Spinach, Mushroom & Olive Scramble

**Serves:** 1-2 · **Time:** 10 min · **Type:** breakfast

## Ingredients
- 3-4 eggs
- 1 cup spinach
- 1/2 cup mushrooms, sliced
- 2 tbsp kalamata olives, chopped
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt & pepper

## Steps
1. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Sauté mushrooms 3-4 min until softened.
2. Add spinach, cook until just wilted, ~1 min.
3. Beat eggs, pour into the pan. Scramble to your preferred doneness.
4. Fold in chopped olives at the end (they just need to warm through). Salt and pepper to taste — go light on salt, the olives carry a lot of it.

## Notes
No dairy needed — the olives give the salty, briny note feta usually would.
