# Eggs & Sautéed Spinach

**Serves:** 1 · **Time:** 8 min · **Type:** breakfast

## Ingredients
- 2 eggs, any style (fried, scrambled, soft-boiled)
- 1-2 cups spinach
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt & pepper, squeeze of lemon optional

## Steps
1. Heat olive oil in a pan, wilt spinach for 1-2 min, season.
2. Cook eggs your preferred way in the same pan or a separate one.
3. Plate together, finish with a squeeze of lemon if you want brightness.

## Notes
The simplest breakfast in the set — good for a busy weekday morning, or as a base you dress up differently each time (avocado, olives, hot sauce).
