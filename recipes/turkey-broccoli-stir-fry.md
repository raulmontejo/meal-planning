# Turkey & Broccoli Stir-Fry

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 15 min (with pre-cooked turkey) · **Type:** dinner

## Ingredients
- 8-10 oz cooked ground turkey (plain, from batch prep)
- 2-3 cups broccoli florets (frozen is fine — pat dry first so it browns instead of steaming)
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced or grated
- 2 tbsp liquid aminos (or coconut aminos)
- 1 tbsp oil for the pan

## Steps
1. Heat oil in a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add broccoli, stir-fry 4-5 min until crisp-tender (longer if using frozen).
3. Add ginger, cook 30 sec until fragrant.
4. Add the cooked turkey and liquid aminos, toss to coat and heat through, 2-3 min.

## Notes
No soy sauce — liquid aminos (or coconut aminos) stands in. Liquid aminos runs saltier, so start with less than you think.
