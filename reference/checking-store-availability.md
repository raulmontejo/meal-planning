# Checking Store Availability Before a Trip

A routine for confirming shopping-list items are actually in stock at a specific store before driving there, rather than finding out in the aisle.

## ALDI (Mayfield Heights)

ALDI doesn't expose a plain item-search stock checker — availability is tied to their pickup flow:

1. Go to `shop.aldi.us` or open the ALDI USA app.
2. Use the store locator to set your pickup store to the **Mayfield Heights** location (confirm it offers Curbside Grocery Pickup — not all locations do).
3. Search or browse for each item on your list and add it to the cart. If it's unavailable at that store, the app/site flags it at this step rather than with a plain in-stock badge.
4. You don't have to complete the order just to check — clearing the cart afterward is fine.

**Caveat:** ALDI's online catalog leans toward what's in their curbside/delivery system, which can be a narrower slice than the physical shelf (their in-store stock rotates with weekly specials/ALDI Finds). Treat an item showing as unavailable online as "double-check in person," not a hard no.

## Heinen's (University Heights) — backup store

Heinen's carries a broader, more consistent catalog, so it's the fallback when ALDI comes up short on something (see `whole-food-swaps.md` for what that's typically been: tahini, whole turkey breast, etc.).

1. Go to `shop.heinens.com` or open the Heinen's app.
2. Set your shopping location to the **University Heights** store.
3. Search each item directly — Heinen's shows real-time availability for the selected store as you browse, without needing to add to cart first.

(Direct automated fetch of Heinen's site was blocked by their bot protection when drafting this — the steps above are the general flow, worth confirming firsthand the first time through.)

## Ethnic grocery stores — for variety, not routine restocking

Worth keeping in mind as a third tier when you want to branch out rather than repeat the same rotation — Middle Eastern, Asian, and Latin markets in the metro area tend to carry things like tahini, coconut aminos, and fresh herbs more cheaply and in better variety than either ALDI or Heinen's, plus ingredients outside what's in `recipes/` entirely. Not part of the weekly-list routine above; more of a "let's try something different" option.
