# Common Bloat Triggers

Notes from working out this meal plan — the biggest dietary culprits behind bloating, and how the recipes here avoid them by default.

- **Onion & garlic (raw or cooked cloves)** — classic gas producers for sensitive guts (high-FODMAP). Recipes here default to garlic-infused oil and scallion greens instead of cloves. Swap back in if this isn't an issue for you.
- **Dairy** — recipes use whole-food stand-ins instead: olives, mashed avocado, toasted walnuts, coconut milk. See `whole-food-swaps.md` for the specific substitutions.
- **Processed snacks** — jerky, bars, and packaged "low-carb" snacks often carry added sugar or sugar alcohols. Snacks in this plan are whole foods: nuts, seeds, olives, boiled eggs, veggies with tahini.
- **Sugar alcohols** — sorbitol, xylitol, erythritol show up in "low-carb" jerky, chocolate, and protein bars. Check labels on anything packaged.
- **Carbonated drinks** — including sparkling water, even though it's carb-free. Worth cutting for a stretch if bloating is a live issue.
- **Large meals / eating fast** — unrelated to ingredients, but often a bigger factor than any single food. Slower, smaller portions help independent of what's on the plate.

## Testing an individual trigger
If bloating persists, an elimination approach works better than guessing: cut the suspected food for ~2 weeks, then reintroduce it alone and watch for a reaction. Trying to eliminate everything at once makes it hard to tell what's actually responsible.
