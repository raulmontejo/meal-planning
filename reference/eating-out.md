# Eating Out

Rules of thumb for a restaurant meal that stays close to the framework in `CLAUDE.md` without needing to brown-bag it.

- **Protein + vegetables as the frame** — grilled, roasted, or seared over fried or breaded.
- **Sauce and dressing on the side** — restaurant versions are often cream- or butter-based, or loaded with onion/garlic (see `bloat-triggers.md`).
- **Skip the bread basket and starchy sides** (fries, rice, pasta) — ask to sub a salad or extra vegetable where possible.
- **Skip soda, beer, and sparkling-water cocktails** — carbonation compounds bloat on top of a heavier meal.
- **Cuisines heavy on raw onion/garlic** (pico, chimichurri, hummus-forward spreads) — ask for those on the side too, same as the oil-infused-instead-of-cloves default elsewhere in this repo.

## When lunch is a wildcard
If lunch is provided and unknown (work event, etc.), pack a light, whole-food backup snack rather than assuming the provided meal will be enough or on-plan — see the journal for an example day.
