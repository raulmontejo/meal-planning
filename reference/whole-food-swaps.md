# Whole-Food Swaps

Substitutions used throughout the recipes here — whole foods rather than processed alternatives (e.g. mashed avocado rather than a dairy-free cream cheese product).

## Dairy
| Instead of | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Feta | Chopped kalamata olives | Similar salty, briny hit |
| Parmesan | Toasted walnuts or pumpkin seeds | For texture/savoriness on salads |
| Goat cheese / cream cheese | Mashed avocado + lemon | Creamy, a little tangy |
| Blue cheese | Toasted walnuts + a mustard-vinegar dressing | Skip the cheese, boost the dressing instead |
| Yogurt | Chilled, whisked canned coconut milk | Or a full chia pudding (see recipes) |
| Butter | Olive oil or avocado oil | For cooking and finishing |

Naturally low-lactose dairy (ghee, aged hard cheeses like parmesan) is often fine even for lactose-sensitive people, if dairy isn't fully off the table — worth testing before cutting it all.

## Red meat
Beef, steak, bacon, and pork are swapped for chicken (breast & thighs), ground turkey, salmon, shrimp, white fish (cod/tilapia), and eggs throughout.

## Pantry staples

**Mustard-vinegar shake** — when a mustard bottle is almost empty, add a splash of red wine vinegar (and a little olive oil if you want it emulsified), shake, and use as an instant vinaigrette. No-waste, no-prep, and covers most of the salads and dressings in this repo (see `cobb-style-chicken-egg-salad.md`, `chicken-sweet-potato-hash.md`). Worth keeping mustard and vinegar both stocked so there's always one bottle heading toward this treatment.

## Aminos
Liquid aminos (Bragg's or similar) works as a 1:1-ish swap for coconut aminos — it's saltier and soy-based, so start with less and adjust. Coconut aminos is soy-free and milder if soy is a concern.

## What's typically at ALDI vs. worth a second stop
**Usually in stock:** chicken, ground turkey, salmon, shrimp, canned tuna, eggs, avocados, olives, walnuts/almonds, olive oil, canned coconut milk, most produce.

**Hit or miss / better at a health-food aisle, Trader Joe's, or an Asian/Middle Eastern grocer:** tahini, coconut aminos, whole roast turkey breast (ALDI tends to carry only deli-sliced turkey — ground turkey or chicken is the easier substitute), sugar-free jerky or snack bars.
